# Roadmap

## Status (2026-10-08)
- **Static site, no backend.** Supabase auth, homework submissions and server-side progress were removed. The code, migrations and skills are kept in `supabase-archive/` for reuse in another project (see its README). Homework is still handed in by sharing a Google Drive folder with the mentor (text in `src/content/weeks/homework/shared.ts`).
- **Content migrated:** BA1 recap, weeks 0–8 and the Final Project page (with homework for weeks 1–8, original PDFs for weeks 0–8, print/save-as-PDF). Week 8 merges the original Week 8 (Application), Week 9 (Error analysis) and Week 10 (Communicating findings) decks into one week (`public/originals/week-08.pdf`, figures in `public/figures/week-08/`): toolbox review, statistics in daily life, sources of error, bias, false positives vs negatives, SE / R² / confusion matrix, report vs presentation, honest charts and a one-page jam stand report, with outside sources cited in the `week-08.ts` header; the Final Project roadmap now ends at Week 8. Week 7 (hypothesis testing) is built from its original deck (`public/originals/week-07.pdf`, images in `public/figures/week-07/`), kept simple (one idea, analogy, small example and recap per section); from section 5 on it uses only jam stand examples (the jam taster, "56 jars a Saturday", the new recipe's spending) with plain-language boxes for H₀, p, α, μ, x̄, σ and z, and easier quick checks (q013–q016), with a short two-groups section and practice CSV `public/data/jam-tasting.csv` for the final-project test. Week 6 (linear regression) uses figures cropped from its original deck (`public/figures/week-06/deck-NN-*.png`), with analogies, worked examples and recaps in every section (`week06Extra.ts`); from section 7 on it uses only jam stand examples (multiple regression on `jam-tasting.csv`, practice "predict jars from visitors"), explains β₀, β₁, p-values and adjusted R² in plain words, and has easier quick checks (q017–q020). Week 5 (Excel data preparation) was authored from the Week 4 / Final Project outline because its deck wasn't available, with practice CSVs in `public/data/`.
- **Progress:** quiz answers and sections read are saved in localStorage (`src/features/progress/localProgress.ts`, key `ba2-progress-v1`). The home page shows each week as a card with its % read, the sidebar ticks read sections, and the Course Contents list shows % per started week. The % counts sections read out of the week's section list, saved the first time the week is opened.
- **Shell:** sidebar with scroll-spy, Course Contents panel, EN/AR/FA + RTL, light/dark theme, code-split routes, GitHub Pages deploy (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`).
